What we do

Full-cycle tooling capabilities, in-house

From metallurgical analysis to the final polished impression, every stage of your die or component is controlled inside our toolroom — so quality is never left to a third party.

Forging & Forming

Hot & cold forging dies built for long runs

We design and manufacture forging dies for high-volume automotive and industrial components, engineered to withstand thermal fatigue and impact load over thousands of strikes.

  • Cylinder head, GM hub and crank-shaft forging dies
  • Rocker arm, end & centre connector tooling
  • Bevel gear, pinion and lug-gear impression dies
  • Mini forging dies for small-batch specialist parts
Precision Machining

CNC milling, turning & EDM on die steels

Our machining cell handles complex geometries and tight tolerances on hardened tool steels, alloy steels and non-ferrous materials — for both tooling and finished components.

  • 3-axis CNC milling for die cavities and cores
  • CNC turning for shafts, bushings and cylindrical parts
  • Wire & sinker EDM for intricate, hard-to-reach features
  • Conventional milling, grinding and lathe work
Metallurgical Analysis

Right steel, right heat-treat, every time

Before a single chip is cut, we analyse the service requirements of your component and select a die steel and heat-treatment that match the load, temperature and wear it will see.

  • Material verification & grade identification
  • Hardness mapping across dies and finished parts
  • Failure analysis on worn or cracked tooling
  • Hardening, tempering & stress-relieving cycles
Die Polishing & Finishing

Mirror finishes that extend die life

Hand polishing with diamond paste, lapping and stoning brings each impression to a mirror finish — improving part release, surface quality and the number of parts a die can produce before service.

  • Diamond-paste polishing to mirror surface
  • Stone finishing for uniform texture where needed
  • Lapping for flat, mating and sealing surfaces
  • Final dimensional inspection & trial fit
Reverse Engineering

No drawing? No problem.

Send us a worn or broken part. We measure, model and manufacture a replacement to original spec — ideal for legacy pumps, obsolete spares and urgent breakdown support.

  • Precision measurement from sample parts
  • CAD reconstruction & manufacturing drawings
  • Pump shafts, impellers, sleeves & casings
  • Fast turnaround for breakdown-critical spares
